## App name (30 max)

Watch Heart: Movie & TV Diary

## Short description (80 max)

Log films, series and anime. Rate, review and share your month as a story.

## Full description (4000 max)

Watch Heart is a diary for everything you watch: the film on Friday, the series you finish on Sunday, the anime you swear is the last episode at 1 am. Log it with half stars and a review, keep a watchlist, and turn each month into a story to share.

EVERY VIEWING, A TICKET
• Each month is a reel of film, and each viewing a ticket stub with the date, poster, stars, heart and review
• Or switch to a poster wall
• Rate with half stars, heart the ones you love, mark rewatches, and hide spoilers behind a tap
• Add who you watched with, where, on what, and up to five vibes

SOMETHING TO WATCH TONIGHT
• Home shows this week’s top titles, picks from what you loved, what’s trending and what’s in cinemas
• In India: FDFS new releases, favourites in your language, and hidden gems
• Choose your country and language, and Home starts with new films in it
• Discover by streaming service, genre, decade, language or country, with seasonal collections
• Can’t decide? Pick for me chooses from your watchlist

TITLE PAGES WITH EVERYTHING
• Synopsis, cast and crew, trailer, seasons, and what to watch next
• IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic scores next to your own stars
• Where to watch in your country: which services stream, rent or sell it
• Tap anyone for their filmography, with what you’ve seen ticked

SERIES AND ANIME, EPISODE BY EPISODE
• Tick off episodes season by season and pick up where you left off
• An Up next widget puts your next episode on your home screen

PLAN A MOVIE NIGHT
• Pick tonight, tomorrow, Saturday or any time, and get a reminder when it’s time

YOUR MONTH AS A STORY
• Any month, or your year, as a sharp 1440 × 2560 story: three layouts, six palettes, ratings, stats or a ranking
• Share a single review, a list or your profile as a story card too
• Share to Instagram, WhatsApp or anywhere, or save to Photos

FRIENDS, IF YOU LIKE
• Share your invite code to see each other’s diaries, reviews and lists
• See which friends have seen a title before you press play
• You choose what friends see. Who you watched with and your tags stay private.

LISTS AND A PROFILE
• Make lists like Best of 2026 or Ghibli ranked, numbered if you like
• Your favourite four, a year of watching day by day, top genres, movie buddies and thirteen achievements

BRING YOUR HISTORY
• Import from Letterboxd, IMDb, Trakt, Moviebase, MyAnimeList, AniList, Netflix, Refract, Serializd and TV Time
• Share a link from IMDb, Letterboxd, TMDB or MyAnimeList and Watch Heart opens it, ready to log
• Export a backup, a spreadsheet (CSV) or a Letterboxd-ready file at any time

SYNC, OR DON’T
• Works fully offline with no account
• An optional free account keeps your diary on every device

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Your diary is never sold
• Optional App lock with your fingerprint, face or screen lock
• A 30-day Trash with undo
• Delete your account and its data at any time, right from the app

Free, with nothing to buy. Watch Heart is one of the Heart Apps, free Android apps by Inigo.

Film and series data and images come from TMDB. Watch Heart uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB. Series data also comes from TVmaze, scores from OMDb, and where-to-watch data from JustWatch through TMDB. Watch Heart is an independent app. It isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic, Letterboxd, Trakt, MyAnimeList, AniList, Netflix, Instagram or WhatsApp.
