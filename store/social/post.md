## LinkedIn

My first app is on the Google Play Store.

Watch Heart is a diary for films, series and anime. You log what you watched with half stars and a review, tick off episodes as you go, check where something is streaming in your country, and turn each month into a story to share.

A few things I cared about while building it:

• Your diary stays yours. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and it works fully offline. The account is optional and only there for sync.
• Coming from another app shouldn't mean starting over, so it imports from Letterboxd, IMDb, Trakt, MyAnimeList, AniList, TV Time and others, and exports to CSV or a Letterboxd file.
• Indian cinema gets proper room: new releases in your language, and where to watch them here.

It's built with Expo and React Native, with film and series data from TMDB. It's free, and it's also on F-Droid and GitHub.

If you try it, I'd like to hear what works and what doesn't.

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.inigocode.watchheart
